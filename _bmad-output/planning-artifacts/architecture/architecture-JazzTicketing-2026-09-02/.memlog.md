- (decision) Region topology: one full stack per region (regional cell). A Property is pinned to exactly one region's cell. Tenant-level records that must span regions (tenant identity, role definitions, corporate rollups) live in a global control plane holding NO guest-identifying data. Prevents cross-region guest data movement while still allowing FR-76 corporate views.
